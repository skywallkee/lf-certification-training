## Three signals, three questions

| Signal | Answers | Typical tool |
|--------|---------|--------------|
| Metrics | Is something wrong, and how much? | Prometheus |
| Logs | What exactly happened? | Fluent Bit, Loki |
| Traces | Where did the time go across services? | Jaeger, OpenTelemetry |

<Callout kind="note" title="Pull, not push">
Prometheus scrapes metrics from targets over HTTP. Short-lived jobs that cannot be scraped push to a
Pushgateway instead.
</Callout>

## Where OpenTelemetry fits

OpenTelemetry is a vendor-neutral set of APIs, SDKs and a collector for producing and shipping all three
signals. It does not store data; a backend does.
