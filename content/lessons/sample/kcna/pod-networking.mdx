## The network model

1. Every Pod gets its own IP address.
2. Pods can reach every other Pod without NAT.
3. Agents on a node can reach every Pod on that node.
4. Containers in one Pod share the IP and reach each other on `localhost`.

<Callout kind="warning" title="Kubernetes does not ship the network">
A CNI plugin such as Calico or Cilium implements these rules. Without one, Pods stay in ContainerCreating.
